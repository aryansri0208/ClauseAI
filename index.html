How it works

Three layers of intelligence

ClauseAI plugs into your existing stack, continuously learns from live traffic, and feeds intelligence back into your infrastructure decisions.

1

Infrastructure instrumentation

ClauseAI integrates with model serving layers, API gateways, and AI workflow logs with lightweight connectors.

  • Token usage and throughput
  • Latency and error patterns
  • Output structure and metadata
  • Per-request and per-workload cost behavior
2

Continuous intelligence

ClauseAI benchmarks models and vendors against real production workloads, not synthetic tests.

  • Hallucination and anomaly detection
  • Performance regressions across releases
  • Cost leakage and unexpected usage
  • Signals of data and model drift
3

Optimization layer

Recommendations flow back into the systems that route traffic and choose models, turning insight into action.

  • Route requests to the right model
  • Switch or diversify vendors
  • Optimize token and context usage
  • Adjust architectures based on reality
